Magigyaru Guide

: For anyone who wants to become a magigyaru :

1. We do not wait to be chosen. We do not transform for permission.
We gather fragments, light, memory, error, softness, and make a form of our own.

2. A magic girl is not innocence. A magic girl is construction. We are built from longing, from attachment, from collected signs, from the small objects that survive every version of the self.

What shines is not purity, but persistence. What transforms is not the body alone, but the will to keep becoming.

This object is not a toy. It is a core. A portable monument to emotional survival. A device for holding softness without surrendering power.

To be a magic girl is to remain permeable, to keep sentiment without shame, to treat decoration as language, and to choose transformation again and again.

Magic is not elsewhere. It is manufactured, carried, worn, protected, and practiced. A magic girl is not chosen. She is formed.
