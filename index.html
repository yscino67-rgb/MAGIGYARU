Magigyaru Guide

: For anyone who wants to become a magigyaru :

: 1 :
We choose.
We do not ask for permission before we transform. Not through someone else’s approval or an exceptional role within a story, but by gathering our values, our memories, and our interests, we shape our own form.

: 2 :
What we create is not a simple transformation. It is a way of reconstructing and expressing the self— taking scattered sensations and emotions, long-held tastes and obsessions, and the small, resilient fragments that have survived through countless versions of who we’ve been, and finally assembling them into a style, a stance, a way of being.

Transformation is not merely a change in appearance. It is not the erasure of who we were, but the act of reappearing in a new form while carrying the many layers accumulated within us.

: 3 :
What shines is not purity, but persistence. Tastes that refuse to fade, feelings we have chosen to hold onto again and again, the will to reach out and rebuild ourselves even after collapse. Through embarrassment, exhaustion, and instability, we continue to refuse the loss of our own sensibility. Our glow does not come from innocence. It comes from surviving, from keeping, from retrieving, from the continuous motion of becoming.

: 4 :
This object is not a toy. This is a core. A device that sustains strength without abandoning softness, a small ritual we create to keep from falling apart, a repeated declaration that we will not give up on ourselves.

The moment it is held, worn, and made visible, this object becomes more than a matter of taste. It becomes a language— one that communicates our emotions, our memories, our attitudes, and our beliefs to the outside world.

: 5 :
Magic does not exist elsewhere. It is not found in distant myths or inherited destinies. It is made, carried, worn, protected, and practiced. Through repeated gestures, through everyday choices, through a sensitivity that refuses to overlook even the smallest details, magic surfaces into reality. It is not a power that is given, but the result of endless collecting, assembling, sensing, and sustaining.

To become a magical girl is to remain permeable, to not be ashamed of emotion, to treat adornment as a language, and to choose, again and again, to keep changing.

: 6 :
We are already within that process. We gather what was broken, what was loved, what was lost, and what we still refuse to let go of, and we rebuild our own core. And with that core carried on our bodies, with a strength that does not abandon softness, with a glow that does not fade even as it trembles, with a presence that insists on revealing itself—we continue becoming magical girls.
